# Impeccable Design & Frontend Standards (معايير التصميم الاحترافي)

Whenever the user asks to **redesign**, **polish**, **style**, **critique**, or **improve** any frontend component, page, or interface:

1. **Automatic Activation of Impeccable (تفعيل تلقائي لمعايير Impeccable)**:
   - Immediately follow the directives in `.agents/skills/impeccable/SKILL.md` and consult relevant playbooks (`reference/craft-floor.md`, `reference/new-work.md`, `reference/polish.md`).
   - Treat every design task as an award-winning design director with peak visual craft, strict attention to information hierarchy, and zero visual clichés.

2. **Elimination of AI Tells (تجنب الأنماط المبتذلة لتصميمات الذكاء الاصطناعي)**:
   - Avoid generic, overused purple-to-blue gradients where they don't belong.
   - Avoid "cards nested inside cards" without clear hierarchical purpose.
   - Ensure high contrast and accessibility (never use muted gray text over saturated colored backgrounds).
   - Use meaningful iconography and intentional whitespace instead of slapping rounded-square icons above every title.

3. **Moroccan Legal & RTL Design Harmony (التناغم مع الهوية البصرية المغربية والعدلية)**:
   - Preserve authentic Moroccan judicial elegance: rich emeralds, royal golds, deep slates, and clean crisp parchment tones.
   - Ensure RTL flow is native and natural, with excellent Arabic typography hierarchy (e.g. `Amiri` for judicial titles, `Cairo` / `Tajawal` for modern readability).

4. **Deterministic Verification (التحقق الآلي من جودة التصميم)**:
   - Run the detector `.agents/skills/impeccable/scripts/impeccable.cmd detect <file>` after significant redesigns to verify absence of detected anti-patterns.
   - Ensure zero regressions on component functionality, state, and TypeScript compilation (`tsc --noEmit`).
